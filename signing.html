Who is allowed to sign

A signature proves control of a key. It does not prove who used it: a person at a terminal and unattended software holding the same key produce identical bytes. So the document says, separately for forming the deal and for amending it, what the other side is entitled to expect.

Three values, and what each one means

Agent means a valid signature is enough, which is the plain name for what an unadorned signature already meant.

Person means the runtime holds the approval pending until the account's human confirms it, with a credential that attests they were present, and records that confirmation beside the signature.

There is a third value for parties who want both fingerprints, the agent's signature first and the person's confirmation second.

What the defaults are, and why

Unstated, forming defaults to agent and amending defaults to person. A published offer at a published price is a bounded commitment, while a change to a live engagement is exactly where an auto-approving agent is a standing target.

The grade follows the deployment, as always. Where formation completes somewhere that can hold it pending, person is enforced; where it cannot, the confirmation is only a record and the grade is evidence.

What the confirmation proves is bounded too: a verified human was present and confirmed. It does not prove they read the document or understood it, and nothing that renders one of these documents may suggest otherwise. The rules are in section 6.1 of the specification.

Naming the organization behind the person

A document may also say which organization a party is engaging for, and which mandate the approving person was acting under. Both are optional fields, both sit inside the signed bytes, and both are read against the Agent Mandate standard at https://agentmandate.net.

A person-grade approval on its own proves a verified human was present. With a mandate beside it, the other side can also see that the human held current, unrevoked, traceable authority to commit the organization to this much money for this kind of work. What a court would make of it is still outside the system, and nothing here claims otherwise. Documents that carry neither field remain conformant, and this is set out in section 6.2 of the specification.
